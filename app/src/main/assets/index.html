🥁 Auto Rhythm

Upload a song (voice + guitar, etc.). The app finds the BPM, the first beat and the chords, then plays beat-aligned drums and a chord-following guitar — offline, free, no server. Drums are synthesized (no copyright packs). Indian taals use real bol patterns (Keherwa, Dadra, Teental, Bhajan, Bhangra) with tabla/dhol-style tones.
